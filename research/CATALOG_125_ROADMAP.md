## Edition gate

1. Find an authoritative, usable source edition; record its exact URL, date, translator, and rights status. For the first six queued titles, local Project Gutenberg plain texts are in `/home/purvals/folio-research-next/` (Alice, Douglass, Frankenstein, Origin, Pride, Time Machine). These files are research inputs only.
2. Follow the original's chapters or argument, selecting both famous and quieter passages. Verify each `quote` against the chosen source and preserve its original wording. Explain unusual terms without rewriting the quotation.
3. Each page needs a clear account of its place in the book, a faithful modern-English meaning, a realistic day-to-day example where the passage supports one, and glossary entries for genuinely difficult words. Fictional events should not be forced into advice.
4. Produce an original, page-relevant image in a style appropriate to that book and inspect it visually. Do not use third-party images, generic stock art, or a cover in place of a page image.
5. Only then move the edition into the live catalog and run the existing publication and image-inventory checks.
