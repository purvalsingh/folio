Good to know

Can I read on iPhone?

Yes. Open the web app in Safari and use Share → Add to Home Screen. The APK is for Android only.

Will the Android download stay current?

Yes. This page checks Folio's latest GitHub release for the current APK. The app also checks for new versions and books.

Do I need an account or internet?

No account is needed. Folio keeps your reading on your device and works offline after content is available. New books and updates need a connection to download.

What kind of books are inside?

Classic works in strategy, philosophy, psychology, Indian wisdom and more. Some newer additions are short introductions while deeper editions are being developed.
